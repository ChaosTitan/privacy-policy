Privacy Policy — VB BOT

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

This bot helps manage SWGOH recruitment posts, moderation, and member roles in the Discord servers where it is installed.

📋 Information Processed

The bot may process Discord information needed for its functions, including your username, user ID, messages in monitored channels, SWGOH.gg guild links, message/channel/timestamp information, and role or membership information. This may also be used for DMs and moderation actions.

Message content is processed only as necessary for the bot's functions and is not intentionally stored in off-platform databases or files.

🌐 SWGOH.gg

When a SWGOH.gg guild link is submitted, the bot may use the SWGOH.gg API to validate the post. API responses are processed temporarily in memory and are not intentionally stored in databases, files, or other persistent off-platform storage.

📝 Recruitment Tracking

Submitted SWGOH.gg guild links may be recorded in a designated Discord channel to prevent duplicate submissions within 24 hours. This information remains within Discord.

🛡️ Moderation Logs

Message removals and moderation actions may be recorded in a designated Discord admin channel, including the user, relevant message/channel information, submitted guild link, reason, and time. These records remain within Discord.

🚫 No Selling

The bot operator does not sell, rent, or provide personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

🔗 Third Parties

The bot operates through Discord and may interact with SWGOH.gg. Those services may process information under their own policies.

📨 Privacy Questions

For privacy questions or requests, contact the server administrators through Discord.

🔄 Changes

This policy may be updated if the bot's data practices change.
